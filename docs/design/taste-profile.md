# Taste profile (from Yannic's 180 ratings)

Source: data/ratings.json, current rating per design. Like = +1, dislike = -1. Love and skip are not used in these ratings. Round 1: 100 designs, mean -0.06. Round 2: 40 designs, mean +0.40. Round 3: 40 designs, mean -0.25. Overall: 90 like, 90 dislike.

Caveat: each family in rounds 2 and 3 has only 5 ratings, and round 1 traits have 8 to 99. Treat any value with fewer than 8 ratings as a hint, not a rule.

## What he likes
- **Outlined cards**: decoration "outlined" +0.75 (n=16), "outline" +0.67 (n=24). The strongest trait with a useful sample.
- **Hello header**: +1.00 (n=8). A greeting line works.
- **Server bars, alerts and energy**: family server +1.00 (n=5), alert +1.00 (n=5), energy +0.60 (n=5). Segmented bars and pill alerts are wanted.
- **Sparklines and line charts**: body spark +0.56 (n=9). Line graphs over text-only sparklines.
- **Rows and comparisons**: body rows +0.56 (n=18), compare +0.50 (n=8). Dot-leader rows and Yannic vs Allyson columns.
- **Round 2 overall**: +0.40 (n=40). Round 2 is the strongest round, and its raster cards are preferred over round 1 text.

## What he dislikes
- **Filled and elevated cards**: filled -0.88 (n=16), elevated -1.00 (n=8).
- **Expressive shapes**: -1.00 (n=5).
- **Text-only sparklines**: sparkText -1.00 (n=10).
- **Big numerals**: big -0.75 (n=8).
- **Frames and wordmark headers**: frame -0.41 (n=17), wordmark -0.38 (n=13).
- **Centred headers**: -0.33 (n=18).
- **Dense tile grids**: tiles -0.29 (n=14).
- **Font B**: -0.62 (n=26). Stay on the normal font.
- **Round 3 Material families**: -0.25 overall. Only outlined variants were not negative.
- **Bold weight**: -0.09 (n=81), which is mild.

## Neutral or unclear
- Morning vs evening: morning 0.00 (n=62), evening +0.08 (n=50). No difference.
- Airy vs dense: airy -0.03, dense +0.04. No clear difference.
- Footer and stars are slightly positive, the other decorations are slightly negative.

## Implications for round 4
1. Outlined cards only. No fills, no elevation.
2. Hello header with a summary line.
3. Modules: server-style segmented bars, energy with a line graph, alerts as outlined pills, rows and Yannic-vs-Allyson comparisons.
4. Numbers at moderate size, not huge. Normal font, not Font B.
5. No expressive shapes, sparkText, frames, wordmarks or big numerals.
