# Taste guide

## 1. Your taste, in short

You like designs that look like a tool: outlined cards, a greeting at the top, bars and rows you can read in one glance, and dot-matrix type. You dislike filled or shadowed cards, huge numbers, decorative frames, and text-only graphs.

- body: segbar: 0.86 (14)

## 2. Rules for designs

1. **Use outlined cards only.** Outlined card +0.83 (36) and outline +0.67 (24). Filled -0.88 (16). Elevated -1.00 (8).
2. **Open with a hello greeting.** Hello header +0.92 (24). Centred headers -0.33 (18). Wordmark headers -0.38 (13).
3. **Show bars, rows and line graphs.** Server +1.00 (5), alert +1.00 (14), rows +0.67 (24), spark +0.56 (9), energy +0.60 (10). Text-only spark -1.00 (10).
4. **Keep numbers moderate.** Big numerals -0.75 (8). Dense tile grids -0.29 (14).
5. **No frames around the whole ticket.** Frame decoration -0.41 (17).
6. **No expressive shapes.** Expressive shapes -1.00 (5). Keep corners small or medium.
7. **Avoid Font B on the printer.** Font B -0.62 (26). Stay with the normal font.
8. **Morning and evening score close together.** Morning +0.10 (69), evening +0.24 (63). Airy +0.07 (114), dense +0.12 (86).

## 7. Recommended design system and six templates

### Design system

- Colour: black, white and one grey for dividers.
- Type: 5x7 dot matrix, the arena top font and pair. Body 2x scale (14 dots). Numerals 3x when one figure matters.
- Cards: outlined, 2-dot stroke, radius 4 or 8 dots, 14-dot padding, 8 to 12 dots between cards.
- Greeting: "HELLO YANNIC" at 3x, then one summary line.
- Data: dot-leader rows, 10-segment bars, 56-dot line graph. No frames, shadows, filled cards or expressive shapes.
