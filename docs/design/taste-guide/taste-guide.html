1. Your taste, in short

You like designs that look like a tool, not a poster: outlined cards, a greeting at the top, bars and rows you can read in one glance, and dot-matrix type. You dislike filled or shadowed cards, huge numbers, decorative frames, and text-only graphs.

2. Rules for designs

Each rule comes from your like and dislike ratings (mean score; +1 like, -1 dislike; count in brackets).

  1. Use outlined cards only. Outlined card +0.83 (36) and outline +0.67 (24). Filled -0.88 (16). Elevated -1.00 (8).
  2. Open with a hello greeting. Hello header +0.92 (24). Centred headers -0.33 (18). Wordmark headers -0.38 (13).
  3. Show bars, rows and line graphs. Server +1.00 (5), alert +1.00 (14), rows +0.67 (24), spark +0.56 (9), energy +0.60 (10). Text-only spark -1.00 (10).
  4. Keep numbers moderate. Big numerals -0.75 (8). Dense tile grids -0.29 (14).
  5. No frames around the whole ticket. Frame decoration -0.41 (17).
  6. No expressive shapes. Expressive shapes -1.00 (5). Keep corners small or medium.
  7. Avoid Font B on the printer. Font B -0.62 (26). Stay with the normal font.
  8. Morning and evening score close together. Morning +0.10 (69), evening +0.24 (63). Airy +0.07 (114), dense +0.12 (86).

7. Recommended design system and six templates

Design system

  • Colour: black, white and one grey for dividers. Nothing else. The printer is 1-bit.
  • Type: 5x7 dot matrix, the arena's top font and pair. Body at 2x scale (14 dots, about 2 mm cap height). Numerals at 3x when a single figure matters.
  • Cards: outlined, 2-dot stroke, corner radius 4 or 8 dots, 14-dot padding, 8 to 12 dots between cards.
  • Greeting: "HELLO YANNIC" at 3x, then one summary line.
  • Data: rows with dot leaders, 10-segment bars, a 56-dot line graph. No frames, shadows, filled cards or expressive shapes.
  • Ornaments: use the arena's top 3 as dividers, sparingly. Keep the rule and the bullets.
